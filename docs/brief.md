# Brief: Glint Host Registry

**Purpose.** Compliance workspace for short-term rental hosts in Europe: registration numbers, night caps, guest reporting (SES Spain, Alloggiati Italy, etc.), tourist tax, evidence packs, listing health, rule radar per city.
**Users.** Individual hosts and small property managers, FR first, then EU cities.
**Status.** Live on Vercel. Growth via city SEO guides (FR/EN) and LinkedIn. Signup source tracked with `?ref=`.
**Owner.** Glint Registries bot; pilot = Money Maker. Board: Notion Projects.
**Key decisions.** One city/country slice at a time, each with playbook, guide, FAQ, landing and footer updates. No legal advice, official sources linked.
**Risks.** Turso drift after merges (post-merge gate). Regulatory facts must be sourced.
